# Contrechamp Privacy Policy

<p class="maj">Last updated: October 7, 2026</p>

Contrechamp is an app for tracking films and series, with a social network built around reviews, published by **{{ site.editeur }}** ({{ site.pays }}), who can be reached at **[{{ site.contact }}](mailto:{{ site.contact }})**. This page explains what data the app uses, why, and how you can control it.

**In short:** you need an account to use Contrechamp. Your diary, ratings, reviews and profile are stored on our servers, and your profile and reviews are visible to other members. No ads, no data selling, no tracking tools. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app, at any time.

## 1. Your account

To create an account, the app asks for:

- your **email address** and a **password** (the password is never stored in plain text: only an encrypted fingerprint is kept);
- a **username**, which other members can see.

On iPhone, you can also **sign in with Apple**: Apple then sends us an identifier, your email (possibly hidden by Apple) and, on your first sign-in, your name if you agree to share it.

You can add a **display name** and a **bio**, and list the **streaming services** you subscribe to (so they are highlighted on title pages).

On your phone, the app only keeps your **sign-in tokens**, in the system's secure storage (Keychain or Keystore). They are not included in phone backups.

## 2. What you record in the app

- your **diary**: the films and episodes you have watched, with their dates;
- your **ratings**, **reviews**, **favourites** and the "contains spoilers" flag;
- the **series you follow** and their status (watching, paused, finished), your **watchlist**;
- the members you **follow**, your **likes** and your **comments**;
- the **accounts you block** and the **reports** you send.

You can edit or delete a diary entry, delete your comments, and remove a series or title from your lists at any time.

## 3. What other members see

Contrechamp is a social network: **your profile is visible to other signed-in members**, with your username, display name, bio, number of followers and accounts followed, your statistics (films watched, time spent on series) and **your diary** (titles watched, ratings, reviews). Your reviews, ratings and viewings may appear in the feed of members who follow you, and your reviews in the "community discovery" section.

**Never visible** to others: your email, your password, your watchlist, your streaming services, the accounts you block and your reports.

A member you block can no longer see your profile or activity, and vice versa. The list of blocked accounts is in *Settings › Blocked accounts*.

## 4. Importing from Letterboxd

If you import your history, the .zip archive you choose is sent to our server, read in memory and then **deleted**: we only keep the viewings, ratings, reviews and watchlist added to your account, plus the list of films that could not be matched (to show it to you).

## 5. Films, series and images (TMDB, JustWatch)

Information about films and series comes from **TMDB** (The Movie Database). Our server queries it on your behalf: TMDB receives neither your account nor your diary. However, **posters and images** are loaded by your phone directly from TMDB's image servers, which therefore see your IP address, as with any website.

The "Where to watch" section uses **JustWatch** data provided by TMDB. If you tap a streaming service logo, the JustWatch page opens in your browser and is then subject to JustWatch's rules.

## 6. Where your data is stored

- The database is hosted by **Supabase**, on servers located in **Paris (France)**.
- The app's server (the API) is hosted by **Render** (Render Services, Inc.). If data passes outside the European Union, this is done under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
- Connections between the app and our servers are **encrypted** (HTTPS).

These providers act only on our behalf. **We do not sell or rent your data**, and the app contains **no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools**.

## 7. How long we keep it

- Your account and everything in it are kept **for as long as your account exists**.
- Sign-in tokens expire after **60 days** without use.
- A review or comment removed by moderation is no longer displayed; the record of the removal and of the report is kept for as long as needed to handle it and prevent abuse.

## 8. Deleting your account {#deleting-your-account}

In the app: *Settings › Delete my account*. Deletion is **immediate and permanent**: your profile, diary, ratings, reviews, comments, likes, follows, lists, blocks and reports are erased from our servers.

If you no longer have access to the app, write to **[{{ site.contact }}](mailto:{{ site.contact }})** from your account's email address, stating your username: your account will be deleted within 30 days at the latest.

## 9. Why we use this data (legal bases)

| Use | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|
| Account, sign-in, diary, series tracking, watchlist | Providing the service you asked for (performance of a contract) |
| Profile, reviews and activity visible to members, follows, comments | Performance of a contract (the core of the social network) |
| Letterboxd import | Performance of a contract, at your request |
| Reports, blocking, moderation, fighting abuse | Legitimate interest in keeping a respectful community, and legal obligations regarding illegal content |
| Account security (password fingerprint, limiting sign-in attempts) | Legitimate interest in protecting accounts |

## 10. Minimum age

Contrechamp is intended for people aged **13 and over**. If you are under 15, talk to a parent before creating an account.

## 11. Your rights

You can **view and correct** your information at any time (*Settings › Edit my profile*) and **delete everything** (section 8). Under the GDPR, you also have the right of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and **portability** (receiving a copy of your data): write to **[{{ site.contact }}](mailto:{{ site.contact }})**. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the **CNIL** ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes)).

## 12. Changes

This policy may change as the app evolves (new feature, new provider, Premium offer). The date at the top of the page shows the latest version; if there is a significant change, the app will let you know.
