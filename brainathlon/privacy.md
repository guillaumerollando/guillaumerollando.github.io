# Brainathlon Privacy Policy

<p class="maj">Last updated: October 8, 2026</p>

Brainathlon is a puzzle game for Android published by **{{ site.editeur }}** ({{ site.pays }}). The data controller is **{{ site.responsable }}**, who can be reached at **[{{ site.contact }}](mailto:{{ site.contact }})**. This page explains what data the game uses, why, and how you can control it.

**In short:** no account, no sign-up, no server. Your progress stays on your phone. After the first 3 free days, the game shows a few ads provided by Google AdMob, which uses data from your device according to your consent choice. You can remove ads with a one-time purchase.

## 1. What stays on your phone

The game stores, **only on your device**:

- your progress (levels, medals, streaks, trophies, game statistics);
- your settings (language, sound, vibration, daily reminder and its time);
- the name you choose for multiplayer games (14 characters at most);
- the date of first launch (to count the free days) and the status of your "no ads" purchase.

This data is **never sent** to the publisher or to any server, and is not backed up to the cloud (Android's automatic backup is turned off for the game). It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

## 2. Multiplayer on the same Wi-Fi

Multiplayer games run **only over your local Wi-Fi network** (or a hotspot), never over the Internet. So that phones can find each other, the game announces the game on that local network with its code, the host's name and the number of players; phones on the same network can also see the host's local network address. Your player name and moves are visible to the other players in the game. These exchanges are not encrypted: play on a network you trust (your Wi-Fi, a friend's, or a hotspot). Nothing is kept after the game, except the medals saved on each phone.

## 3. Daily reminder

The reminder is off by default: the game offers it after your first event. If you turn it on, it is a **local notification** scheduled by your phone (at a time you choose, or computed by the game from the times you usually play, which are remembered on the device). Android asks for your permission before the first reminder. No data leaves the device, and reminders stop if you don't open the game for a week. You can turn it off at any time in *Profile › Settings* or in Android settings.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

There are no ads during the first 3 days. After that, the game shows short ads at natural breaks (never during a game), provided by **Google AdMob** (Google Ireland Limited). No ads are shown offline.

To serve and measure these ads, Google may collect:

- your device's advertising ID;
- your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
- technical information (device model, Android version, language);
- your interactions with ads (views, clicks).

**Your choice:** if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form (Google's) is shown before any ad. If you decline, ads remain **non-personalized**: Google then uses limited data, notably to fight fraud. You can change your mind at any time in *Profile › Settings › Privacy*. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (*Google › Ads*).

Google acts on its own behalf for advertising and may process this data outside the European Union, with the safeguards provided by its own rules: [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). The publisher only receives overall statistics from Google (views, revenue), no data about you.

## 5. "No ads" purchase

The one-time purchase that removes ads is handled by **Google Play**. The publisher never sees your payment information: Google only confirms to the game that the purchase was made, and the game remembers it on your phone. The "Restore purchases" button retrieves it from Google Play after a reinstall.

## 6. Crash reports

If you agreed to share diagnostic data with Google, Android and Google Play may send the publisher **anonymous** crash reports (phone model, Android version, where the crash happened) through the Google Play Console. They are used only to fix bugs.

## 7. Android permissions

The game asks for: network access, network and Wi-Fi state (multiplayer and ads), vibration (touch feedback, adjustable), showing notifications (reminder, with your consent) and restarting the reminder after your phone reboots. It does not access your contacts, precise location, microphone, camera or files.

## 8. Why we use this data (legal bases)

| Use | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|
| Progress, settings, multiplayer, reminder | Running the game you asked for (performance of a contract) |
| Personalized ads | Your consent |
| Non-personalized ads, fraud prevention | The publisher's legitimate interest in funding the free game |
| "No ads" purchase | Performance of the purchase contract |
| Crash reports | Legitimate interest in fixing bugs |

## 9. Children

Brainathlon is for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## 10. Your rights

Since the game's data stays on your phone, you control it directly: to erase it, uninstall the game or clear its data. For advertising data, you can withdraw consent in the game and manage your advertising ID in Android.

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to and restrict processing. For any question: **[{{ site.contact }}](mailto:{{ site.contact }})**. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the **CNIL** ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr/en/home)), or your local authority.

## 11. Changes

This policy may change as the game evolves (new feature, new partner). The date at the top shows the latest version; the game will let you know about any significant change.
