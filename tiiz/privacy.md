# Tiiz Privacy Policy

<p class="maj">Last updated: November 24, 2026</p>

Tiiz is a party game for Android published by **{{ site.editeur }}** ({{ site.pays }}). The data controller is **{{ site.editeur }}**, reachable at **[{{ site.contact }}](mailto:{{ site.contact }})**. This page explains what data the game uses, why, and how you can control it.

**In short:** no account, no sign-up, no server. Names and settings stay on your phone; multiplayer only goes through your local Wi-Fi. The game shows a short ad provided by Google AdMob when a game starts, which uses data from your device depending on your consent choice. You can remove ads with a one-time purchase.

## 1. What stays on your phone

The game saves **only on your device**:

- the players' names from the last game, and the mood and number of rounds you chose;
- the game in progress (players, scores, rounds played), so you can resume it if the app closes;
- questions and content already seen (to avoid repeats) and rules already explained;
- your settings (sound, vibration) and the state of your "no ads" purchase.

This data is **never sent** to the publisher or to any server, and is not backed up to the cloud (Android's automatic backup is turned off for the game). It is deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

## 2. Multiplayer on the same Wi-Fi

Games with several phones run **only over your local Wi-Fi network** (or a hotspot), never over the Internet. So that phones can find each other, the game announces the game on that local network with its code, the table's name and the number of players; phones on the same network can also see the local network address of the phone hosting the game. Names, answers, votes and drawings are visible to the other players in the game, just like around a table. These exchanges are not encrypted: play on a network you trust (your Wi-Fi, a friend's, or a hotspot). Nothing is kept after the game, except the game in progress on the hosting phone (so it can be resumed).

## 3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows a short ad (about 5 seconds) when a game starts, at most once every 8 minutes. Never when the app opens, never during a round, and never on the phone of a player who joins someone else's game. It is provided by **Google AdMob** (Google Ireland Limited). When you are offline, no ad is shown.

To display and measure these ads, Google may collect:

- your device's advertising ID;
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
- technical information (model, Android version, language);
- your interactions with ads (impressions, clicks).

**Your choice:** if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form (Google's) is shown before any ad. If you decline, ads remain **non-personalised**: Google then uses limited data, notably for fraud prevention. You can change your mind at any time from the game's *No ads* screen (*Privacy (ads)* button). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (*Google › Ads*).

Google acts here on its own behalf for advertising and may process this data outside the European Union, under the safeguards provided by its own rules: [how Google uses data from partner sites and apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). The publisher only receives aggregate statistics from Google (impressions, revenue), no data about you.

## 4. "No ads" purchase

The one-time purchase that removes ads is handled by **Google Play**. The publisher never sees your payment information: Google only confirms to the game that the purchase was made, and the game remembers it on your phone. The "Restore purchases" button (*No ads* screen) finds it again with Google Play after a reinstall or on a new phone.

## 5. Crash reports

If you agreed to share diagnostic data with Google, Android and Google Play may send the publisher **anonymous** crash reports (phone model, Android version, location of the crash) through the Google Play Console. They are used only to fix bugs.

## 6. Android permissions

The game asks for: network access, network and Wi-Fi state (multiplayer, ads and purchase) and vibration (touch feedback, adjustable). Some rounds use the phone's motion sensor (tilting or shaking it): these movements are read on the spot and never recorded. The game does not access your contacts, precise location, microphone, camera or files, and sends no notifications.

## 7. Why this data (legal bases)

| Use | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|
| Names, game in progress and settings | Operating the game you asked for (performance of a contract) |
| Multiplayer over local Wi-Fi | Operating the game you asked for (performance of a contract) |
| Personalised ads | Your consent |
| Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention | Publisher's legitimate interest in funding the free game |
| "No ads" purchase | Performance of the purchase contract |
| Crash reports | Legitimate interest in fixing bugs |

## 8. Age and content

Tiiz is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. It is a party game with humour that can be a little cheeky; it has no real-money gambling and no sexual content, and does not promote alcohol.

## 9. Your rights

Since the game's data stays on your phone, you control it directly: erasing it = uninstalling the game or clearing its data. For advertising data, you can withdraw your consent in the game and manage your advertising ID in Android.

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, objection and restriction. For any question: **[{{ site.contact }}](mailto:{{ site.contact }})**. You may also lodge a complaint with the **CNIL** ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr/en/make-a-complaint)).

## 10. Changes

This policy may change with the game (new feature, new partner). The date at the top shows the latest version; for any significant change, the game will let you know.
