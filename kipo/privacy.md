# Kipo Privacy Policy

<p class="maj">Last updated: October 9, 2026</p>

Kipo is an Android and iOS app to organise events and chat in groups, published by **{{ site.editeur }}** ({{ site.pays }}). The data controller is **{{ site.responsable }}**, who can be reached at **[{{ site.contact }}](mailto:{{ site.contact }})**.

**In short:** you need an account (email, Google or Apple). Your messages are end-to-end encrypted: we cannot read them. They are erased from our servers after 7 days. No ads, no usage analytics, no selling of data. You can delete your account at any time from the app or at [kipo-app.fr/delete-account](https://kipo-app.fr/delete-account).

## 1. Your account data

- **Identity:** email address, first and last name, profile picture (optional). Your email is never shown to other users.
- **Sign-in:** password (stored hashed by our authentication provider, never in clear) or Google / Apple identifier.
- **Your activity in the app:** events you create or join, invitation replies, groups, favourites, people met by QR code, users you blocked, reports you made.
- **Device:** notification token (to send you alerts), IP address and device type in the server's technical logs.

## 2. Your messages and media

- Messages (text, voice, photos, videos) are **encrypted on your phone** before sending (AES-256-GCM, keys shared between members). We only store data we cannot read.
- **Everything is erased from our servers at most 7 days after sending.** Messages you *save* stay only on the phones of the members who received them.
- Your private encryption key is kept in a vault protected by your PIN: we do not know your PIN. If you forget it, we cannot recover your old messages.
- Android automatic backup is turned off for Kipo.

## 3. Reporting and blocking

You can block a user (they can no longer message you privately) or report them. A report contains the reason, your optional comment and, if you choose to attach them, a few messages you decrypted yourself. It is reviewed by the publisher and is not shared with the reported person. We may suspend or delete an account that breaks the [terms](terms).

## 4. Who processes your data (processors)

| Provider | Role | Data |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** (database and authentication, servers in France — Paris region; file storage) | Data hosting and sign-in | Account, events, groups, encrypted messages, profile pictures and encrypted media |
| **Render** | Application server hosting | All requests go through it (IP address, technical logs) |
| **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** / **Apple Push** | Sending notifications | Notification token, generic alert text (never message content) |
| **Google / Apple** | "Sign in with…" (optional) | Identifier and email of the chosen account |
| **event-to-calendar.com** | "Add to calendar" button (optional) | Title, description and location of the event you add |

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union, under contractual safeguards (European Commission standard clauses). We sell no data and show no ads.

## 5. Android / iOS permissions

- **Camera, microphone:** take a photo, send a voice message, scan a QR code.
- **Photos and videos:** pick an image to send; save a received media to your gallery.
- **Notifications:** be told about a message, an invitation or an announcement.

None of these permissions is used in the background for other purposes. Kipo does not access your contacts, location or SMS.

## 6. Why this data (legal bases)

| Use | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|
| Account, events, groups, messages | Providing the service you asked for (contract) |
| Notifications | Contract; you can turn them off in your phone |
| Technical logs, security, abuse prevention, reports | Publisher's legitimate interest in protecting the platform and its users |
| Add to calendar | Your choice each time (consent) |
| Legal obligations (court order) | Legal obligation |

## 7. How long

| Data | Duration |
|---|---|
| Messages and media sent | 7 days at most on our servers |
| Events | Deleted 7 days after they end; "Souvenirs" (photos) stay only on your phone |
| Account and profile | Until you delete the account |
| Technical logs | Set by the host (a few weeks at most) |
| Reports | As long as needed for platform safety |

## 8. Deleting your account

*Profile › Modifier mon profil › Supprimer mon compte*, or the page [kipo-app.fr/delete-account](https://kipo-app.fr/delete-account) if you no longer have the app. Your access, name, email, picture, keys and links to groups and events are erased. The page lists what may remain.

## 9. Minimum age

Kipo is for people aged **15 and over**. If you think a younger child has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

## 10. Your rights

You can access, correct, erase, export, restrict or object to the processing of your data by writing to [{{ site.contact }}](mailto:{{ site.contact }}). We answer within one month. You can also complain to the **CNIL** ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)).

## 11. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and, for an important change, the app informs you.
