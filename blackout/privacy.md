# Jimmy's Blackout Privacy Policy

<p class="maj">Last updated: October 9, 2026</p>

Jimmy's Blackout is a card game for Android published by **{{ site.editeur }}** ({{ site.pays }}). The data controller is **{{ site.responsable }}**, reachable at **[{{ site.contact }}](mailto:{{ site.contact }})**. This page explains what data the game uses, why, and how you can control it.

**In short:** no account, no sign-up, no server. Your progress stays on your phone. The game shows a short ad provided by Google AdMob at the start of a run, which uses data from your device depending on your consent choice. You can remove ads with a one-time purchase.

## 1. What stays on your phone

The game saves **only on your device**:

- your progress (runs in progress, unlocked cards, relics and outfits, scores, statistics);
- your settings (language, sound, music, vibration);
- the state of your "no ads" purchase.

This data is **never sent** to the publisher or to any server. However, Android's automatic backup may copy the game's data to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone: that copy is managed by Google according to your Android settings (*Google › Backup*), and the publisher has no access to it. The data is deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

## 2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows a short ad (about 5 seconds) at the start of a run, never when the app opens and never during a fight. It is provided by **Google AdMob** (Google Ireland Limited). When you are offline, no ad is shown.

To display and measure these ads, Google may collect:

- your device's advertising ID;
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
- technical information (model, Android version, language);
- your interactions with ads (impressions, clicks).

**Your choice:** if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form (Google's) is shown before any ad. If you decline, ads remain **non-personalised**: Google then uses limited data, notably for fraud prevention. You can change your mind at any time in the game's settings (*Privacy*). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (*Google › Ads*).

Google acts here on its own behalf for advertising and may process this data outside the European Union, under the safeguards provided by its own rules: [how Google uses data from partner sites and apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). The publisher only receives aggregate statistics from Google (impressions, revenue), no data about you.

## 3. "No ads" purchase

The one-time purchase that removes ads is handled by **Google Play**. The publisher never sees your payment information: Google only confirms to the game that the purchase was made, and the game remembers it on your phone. The "Restore purchases" button finds it again with Google Play after a reinstall.

## 4. Crash reports

If you agreed to share diagnostic data with Google, Android and Google Play may send the publisher **anonymous** crash reports (phone model, Android version, location of the crash) through the Google Play Console. They are used only to fix bugs.

## 5. Android permissions

The game requests: network access and network state (ads and purchase) and vibration (haptic feedback, adjustable). It does not access your contacts, precise location, microphone, camera or files, and sends no notifications.

## 6. Why this data (legal bases)

| Use | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|
| Progress and settings | Operating the game you asked for (performance of a contract) |
| Personalised ads | Your consent |
| Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention | Publisher's legitimate interest in funding the free game |
| "No ads" purchase | Performance of the purchase contract |
| Crash reports | Legitimate interest in fixing bugs |

## 7. Age and content

Jimmy's Blackout is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. The game humorously depicts a drunken party and includes references to cannabis; it has no real-money gambling and no sexual content.

## 8. Your rights

Since the game's data stays on your phone, you control it directly: erasing it = uninstalling the game or clearing its data. For advertising data, you can withdraw your consent in the game and manage your advertising ID in Android.

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, objection and restriction. For any question: **[{{ site.contact }}](mailto:{{ site.contact }})**. You may also lodge a complaint with the **CNIL** ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr/en/make-a-complaint)).

## 9. Changes

This policy may change with the game (new feature, new partner). The date at the top shows the latest version; for any significant change, the game will let you know.
